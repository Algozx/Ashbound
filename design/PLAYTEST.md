# Playtest checklist

Run after every milestone, in a real desktop browser. Note the browser and the commit.

## Fun
- [ ] I understood what to do within 30 seconds without reading.
- [ ] I played for 5 minutes or more and wanted another run.
- [ ] Every attack, hit and dodge gave feedback I could feel.

## Readability
- [ ] Enemy attacks are readable before they land.
- [ ] No overlapping UI, clipping or placeholder text.

## Stability and performance
- [ ] No console errors during 10 minutes of play.
- [ ] Holds 60 FPS after 10 minutes.
- [ ] Pause, death and restart work in the build, not only in the Editor.
